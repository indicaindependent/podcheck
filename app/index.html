What each factor measures

  • Code destination 35 pts — the QR is resolved server-side. A Metrc Retail ID that the state registry recognises scores in full; a code the registry has never issued, or one pointing somewhere else, caps the score.
  • Inside of the pouch 12 pts - pouch products — you answer. Genuine turn pouches carry the turn logo pattern on the inside foil; plain foil caps the score at 40.
  • Compliance sticker position 8 pts - pouch products — you answer. Genuine units carry the state sticker on the back label; a sticker on the front caps at 44, no sticker caps at 60.
  • Device vs pouch artwork 5 pts — turn prints a pod (with "pen sold separately") on pod pouches and a pen on all-in-one pouches; the artwork must match what is inside.
  • Barcode vs turn's asset hub 10 pts — the UPC under the barcode is looked up against the barcodes turn publishes for its own products; a real barcode on the wrong flavor or device is a copied-label tell.
  • Flavor, size and effect vs turn's catalog 10 pts — the name and the three short lines under it are matched against turn.me's own listing (142 products, four states, September 2026). A name that isn't listed proves nothing on its own; tags that belong to a different genuine flavor cap the score at 30.
  • Compliance label text 15 pts — text read from your back-label photo, checked against California panel rules (warning symbol, THC, licence format, dates). CA rules only for now.
  • Seller licence 10 pts — the licence number or store name you enter is looked up live in the California DCC database. New York OCM lookup is not wired yet.
  • Purchase context 5 pts — where you bought it and what you paid.

There is no image-recognition "visual match" factor. PodCheck does not judge print quality or colour from photos; every factor above is either a registry lookup, a text match against turn's published catalog and barcodes, or an answer you give about the physical package.

Factors that are unavailable (no photo, no licence entered, wrong device for the check) are excluded from the denominator rather than scored zero.

Sources

  • State registries: Metrc Retail ID landing pages for New York and California.
  • turn's catalog and barcodes: turn.me listings and turn's official public asset hub (pulled Sep 7 2026).
  • Pouch tells: turn's own account answering "is this real?" posts, and turn's 2025 budtender guide.

Privacy

Photos are analyzed in-memory for a check and are not stored. Photos are only stored on our servers if you explicitly build a report and check the consent box, so they can be attached for you.
